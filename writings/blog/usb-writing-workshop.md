A great experience I had in the _Universidad Simón Bolívar_ was a Writing Workshop organized by a couple of Literature students from the _Universidad Central de Venezuela_ as required community service for their degree. The main goal was something I was not interested in, but the writing exercises were a phenomenal experience.

Reading your own writings in front of strangers is quite a challenge for someone like me, but as I got to listen to marvelous writings by the other people present, I think it was a fair trade. There are quite excellent writers hidden in the science aisles.

I started writing (in Spanish) on a little Medium site, for anyone who's interested. Made-up abstract stories are most of what you'll encounter there.
