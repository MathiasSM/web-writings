Together with Carlos Serrada, a friend in the same Language Theory class (Official name: _Traductores e Interpretadores_), we made a Ruby interpreter for the "Retina" language, made up by Prof. David Lilue. With it, you can programmatically draw simple bitmaps with a simple syntax. 

We called the Ruby interpreter for Retina language _[Retyna](https://retyna.github.io/)_, and made that website for its code and documentation.
